# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11/20/2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Chapman Products Co., which owns and operates Fattixx ("Fattixx," "we," "us" or "our") collects, uses and shares personal information when you use fattixx.com, our apps, our check-in tools and related services (the "Services"), whether you are browsing events, buying tickets or hosting events. It is part of our [Terms of Service](/terms).

**Organizers have their own privacy practices.** When you buy a ticket or register for an event, the organizer receives your information and uses it under its own privacy policy, as described in section 5. This policy covers what Fattixx does with your information, not what organizers do.

**We do not sell your personal information for money.** We share it only as described in section 4.

## 1. Information we collect

**Information you give us:**

| Who | What we collect |
| --- | --- |
| Everyone with an account | Name, email address, phone number, sign-in method (email code, text code or Google), and any profile details you add. |
| Buyers | Order details (event, ticket types, quantity and price), attendee names you enter, answers to questions the organizer asks at checkout, ticket transfers, refund requests and waitlist sign-ups. |
| Organizers | Organizer name and logo, event details, images and videos, venue addresses, ticket settings and messages you send to attendees. |
| Organizers receiving payouts | Legal name, business details, date of birth, address, tax identification number and bank account details. Most of this goes directly to Stripe for identity verification and payouts; we keep only what we need, such as the last four digits of a bank account. |
| Anyone who contacts us | The content of messages to our support team, reports about events, and survey responses. |

**Payment cards.** Card details are entered into and stored by Stripe, our payment processor. Fattixx does not store full card numbers. We receive limited information such as the card brand, last four digits, expiration date and billing ZIP code.

**Information we collect automatically:**

- device and browser information, IP address, and app or browser identifiers

- the pages and events you view, your searches, clicks and purchases, and the website that referred you

- approximate location based on your IP address, and precise location only if you allow it in your browser or device settings, which we use to show events near you

- ticket check-in data, such as when a ticket was scanned

- information from cookies, browser storage and similar technologies, as described in section 6. For example, an event you begin creating is saved in your browser before you sign up, so you don't lose your work.

**Information from other sources:**

- Google, if you sign in with Google (your name, email address and profile picture)

- Stripe, about identity verification status, payouts, refunds and payment disputes

- organizers, when they add attendees, complimentary tickets or guest lists

- other users who transfer a ticket to you or buy a ticket in your name

- fraud-prevention, analytics and advertising partners, and public sources such as social media profiles organizers link to

**Sensitive information.** We prohibit organizers from using ordinary custom checkout questions, uploads or messages to request Social Security numbers, payment card or bank numbers, government identification documents or numbers, medical diagnoses, or other sensitive personal information. Organizers must contact us before using the Services for any legally required or accessibility-related sensitive information; any permitted collection must use an expressly approved, purpose-specific process with appropriate notices, consents and safeguards. If sensitive information is submitted despite these restrictions, we may process it to secure the Services, respond to the request, or remove or restrict the information as appropriate; this does not authorize organizers to collect it. We collect organizers' tax identification numbers and identity details only for legally required verification and payouts. We use precise location only with your permission.

## 2. How we use information

- **To provide the Services:** create and secure accounts, send sign-in codes, process orders and payments, deliver tickets, run check-in, handle transfers, refunds and waitlists, and pay organizers.

- **To show you relevant events:** show events near you or in the city you choose, remember your filters, and recommend events based on what you have viewed or purchased.

- **To communicate with you:** send order confirmations, tickets, event reminders and changes, organizer messages you are entitled to receive, account and policy notices, and support responses; and, if you allow it, marketing about events and Fattixx features.

- **To keep people safe:** verify organizers; prevent fraud, automated purchasing, abuse and chargebacks; enforce our Terms; and protect the security of the Services.

- **To improve Fattixx:** analyze how the Services are used, fix problems and develop new features. We may create de-identified or aggregated information, such as the number of tickets sold in a city, and use it for any lawful purpose. Where applicable law requires, we take reasonable measures to prevent association with an individual, maintain and use de-identified information in de-identified form, do not attempt to reidentify it except as legally permitted to test de-identification, and contractually require recipients to comply with applicable de-identification obligations.

- **To advertise:** promote events and Fattixx on other websites and apps, and measure how our ads perform, as described in section 6.

- **To comply with the law:** meet tax, accounting and payment-network requirements, respond to lawful requests, and establish or defend legal claims.

## 3. Our legal responsibilities to organizers and buyers

Fattixx acts as an independent controller or business for personal information it uses for its own account, payment, fraud prevention, security, recommendations, analytics, advertising, legal and platform purposes. Organizers are independent controllers for attendee information they use for their own events. Fattixx may act as a processor or service provider for information processed solely on an organizer's documented instructions to provide organizer functions, such as permitted custom checkout answers, attendee-list tools and authorized organizer messaging. The role depends on the particular processing activity, not simply on who collected the information; organizer-directed processing is governed by section 6.9 of the Terms and any applicable data-processing addendum. We process organizers' information to provide the Services to them, to verify their identity and to pay them. We keep the information we need to meet our legal obligations, including tax and payment-network rules.

To ask an organizer what information it holds about you, or to stop receiving its messages, contact the organizer directly or use the unsubscribe link in its message. If you cannot reach an organizer, email [info@fattixx.com](mailto:info@fattixx.com) and we will help.

**If you are an organizer,** you are responsible for providing a privacy notice where the law requires one, responding to attendees' privacy requests, keeping exported attendee lists secure, and deleting attendee information when you no longer need it.

## 6. Cookies, analytics, advertising and location

**Cookies and similar technologies.** We use cookies, browser storage, pixels and software development kits to:

- keep you signed in and keep your cart, filters and event drafts (strictly necessary)

- remember your settings, such as the city you selected (preferences)

- understand how the Services are used (analytics)

- show and measure ads for Fattixx and for events on other websites and apps (advertising)

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, although some features, such as staying signed in, may not work without them. Where the law requires, we ask for your consent before using non-essential cookies. We treat a Global Privacy Control signal from your browser as a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information and targeted advertising for that browser, and apply it to an associated account where required by law. The "Your Privacy Choices" control in section 9 lets you submit or change these advertising choices. An opt-out stops the applicable advertising disclosures; it does not prevent necessary processing for purchases, security or other permitted purposes. Clearing browser data or using another browser or device may require you to set your preferences again unless an applicable account-level choice applies.

**Location.** We use your IP address to estimate your city. If you choose "Use my current location," your browser shares your precise location so we can show events near you. We use it only for that purpose and do not keep a history of your precise location. You can turn off location access in your browser or device settings at any time.

## 7. Emails and text messages

**Account and order messages,** such as sign-in codes, receipts, tickets, event changes and cancellations, and policy notices, may be sent while you have an account or open orders. You can revoke consent to text messages; required account or order notices may instead be delivered by email or in the Services through permitted channels.

**Marketing from Fattixx** is sent by email, and by text message only if you have agreed to receive marketing texts. Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link.

**Text messages.** If you sign in with your phone number, we text you one-time sign-in codes. If you agree to receive text messages about events, you may receive recurring automated messages. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for help, or contact info@fattixx.com to revoke text-message consent. We honor other reasonable revocation methods as required by law. Revoking text consent may prevent phone-code sign-in; another supported sign-in method may be used. Agreeing to marketing text messages is not a condition of any purchase.

**Organizer messages.** Organizers can message you through Fattixx about events you purchased tickets for, and can send you marketing only if you agreed to hear from them. Organizer-specific email preferences generally apply to that organizer. Text-message opt-outs may apply more broadly to messages from the same sender or number where applicable law requires. Organizers must honor applicable opt-outs in their outside messaging tools as well; contact them directly about messages sent outside Fattixx. We retain consent and suppression records as reasonably needed to document choices and prevent further unauthorized marketing.

## 8. Retention and security

**How long we keep information.** We keep personal information for as long as your account is open and as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, and then delete or de-identify it. The table below describes our standard retention schedule, subject to the limited exceptions explained after it.

| Information | Retention period |
| --- | --- |
| Account details | While your account is open, and up to 90 days after you close it |
| Orders, payouts, refunds and tax records | 7 years, to meet tax, accounting and payment-network requirements |
| Event listings | While the organizer's account is open; past events remain in buyers' order histories |
| Event drafts saved in your browser | Until you publish the event, start over or clear your browser data |
| Support messages and fraud-prevention records | 3 years, or longer if needed to resolve a dispute |
| Analytics and advertising data | Up to 26 months |

**Retention exceptions.** We may retain the information reasonably necessary for a legal obligation or hold, tax or accounting requirement, an unresolved refund or dispute, fraud prevention, security, enforcement, or documenting consent and opt-outs. Any longer retention must be limited to that purpose and the period reasonably necessary or legally required. Deleted information may remain in restricted backups until the ordinary backup cycle removes it; we do not use it for ordinary business purposes and reapply deletion when restoring a backup, except where lawful retention is required. Organizers, Stripe and other independent recipients control their own copies under their own notices and legal obligations. These exceptions do not permit indefinite retention of information no longer needed for a lawful purpose.

**Security.** We use safeguards such as encryption in transit, access controls, and payment processing by a PCI-compliant provider. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee the security of your information. Because sign-in uses one-time codes, please keep your email account and phone secure. If a security breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the appropriate authorities as required by law.

- close your account

Where no statutory deadline applies, we aim to respond within 45 days. If an extension is permitted and needed, we will tell you why and when to expect a response. We may need to keep certain information after a deletion request, such as order and tax records, and deleting your account does not delete information organizers have already received.

**U.S. state privacy rights.** Residents of states with consumer privacy laws, including California, Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Indiana, Iowa, Kentucky, Maryland, Minnesota, Montana, Nebraska, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Oregon, Rhode Island, Tennessee, Texas, Utah and Virginia, and other states as their laws take effect, may have the right to:

- know what personal information we collect, use, share and sell, and obtain a copy in a portable format

- correct or delete their personal information

- opt out of the sale of personal information, sharing for targeted advertising, and profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects

- limit the use of sensitive personal information

- appeal our decision on a request, by replying to our decision or emailing [info@fattixx.com](mailto:info@fattixx.com) with "Appeal" in the subject line. If you disagree with the outcome of your appeal, you may contact your state attorney general.

You may use an authorized agent to make a request. We may ask the agent for proof of authorization and verify your identity directly. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

**Notice to California residents.** The categories of personal information we collect, and have collected during the preceding 12 months to the extent the relevant Services have operated, include: identifiers; customer records; commercial information, such as orders; internet and other electronic network activity; approximate geolocation and, with your permission, precise geolocation; inferences about event interests; and, for organizers, financial and tax information used for verification and payouts. We may "share" identifiers and internet activity with advertising partners for cross-context behavioral advertising, and you may opt out as described above.
